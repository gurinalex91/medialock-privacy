MediaLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

What MediaLock does

MediaLock displays playback controls for active media sessions on supported lock-screen widget hosts. An account is not required.

Notification Access and media information

Android's Notification Access is a broad system capability. MediaLock uses it to discover active MediaSessions and update playback controls. The app processes the source application's package and display name, track title and artist, artwork, and playback state. It does not use notification text or content. Media information is processed on this device to display the player and send the playback action you choose.

Local storage and backup

MediaLock stores discovered source package names, display labels, last-seen times and cached source-app icons in private app storage. It also stores your widget appearance settings and source enabled/disabled choices. Track metadata, artwork and playback state are not saved as listening history.

Android device-to-device transfer may restore your widget appearance and enabled/disabled source preferences. MediaLock excludes the discovered-player registry and icon cache from transfer and does not use Android cloud backup.

Network, analytics, advertising, and sharing

MediaLock does not use a backend, analytics, advertising SDKs, or network connections to transmit your media or notification information. MediaLock does not transmit media or notification information off your device or share it with third parties. Android system services and media-player apps operate under their own privacy terms.

Deleting local data and revoking access

Clearing MediaLock storage or uninstalling the app removes its local preferences, discovered-source registry, and cached icons from that installation. Notification Access can be revoked in Android Settings. Revoking it prevents MediaLock from discovering active media sessions until access is granted again.
